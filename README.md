## 📖 Abstract

This project presents the design study of a **wooden oudh burner stand**, a pedestal-style product designed to support a metal receptacle for aromatic incense.

The product consists of a circular wooden upper body, a shaped central stem, a broad circular base, and a metal bowl seated in the top opening. Decorative circumferential grooves and the natural appearance of the wood contribute to the product's visual character.

The project applies Engineering Drawing concepts such as:

- Symmetry
- Centre-line representation
- Circular profiles
- Orthographic projection
- Front and top views
- Sectional representation
- Dimensioning
- Component identification
- Product geometry
- Material selection
- Engineering representation

The wooden structure functions as a **support**, while the metal bowl functions as the heat-containing receptacle. The wooden structure should **not be treated as a combustion chamber**.

The dimensions presented in this project are proposed design values rather than measured dimensions of the photographed product. Actual dimensions, material specifications, joining details, thermal protection, and performance must be verified before manufacturing or heated operation. 1

## 🏗️ Product Overview

The Wooden Oudh Burner Stand is a pedestal-style support structure consisting of four major components:

### 1. Upper Wooden Housing

The upper portion is a rounded circular wooden body with decorative external grooves.

**Function:**
- Supports the metal bowl.
- Surrounds the metal receptacle.
- Provides the main upper structural body.
- Provides decorative appearance.

### 2. Central Stem

The central stem is a narrow, shaped wooden support connecting the upper housing and the base.

**Function:**
- Transfers the supported weight from the upper housing to the base.
- Provides the pedestal structure.
- Contributes to the overall appearance of the product.

### 3. Circular Base

The lower section forms a broad, flared circular base.

**Function:**
- Provides the supporting footprint.
- Distributes the load onto the supporting surface.
- Helps improve overall stability.

### 4. Metal Bowl / Insert

An open metal receptacle is seated inside the upper wooden opening.

**Function:**
- Acts as the heat-containing receptacle.
- Holds the aromatic material.
- Separates the burning material from the wooden support.

The metal bowl visible in the photographs contains dark residue, indicating previous use. However, the photographs do not establish its alloy, thickness, temperature capability, or thermal protection arrangement. 4

## 🧱 Material Selection

### Wooden Body

For a similar model, a suitable material would be:

**Sound, seasoned solid wood**

The selected wood should ideally:

- Be properly seasoned.
- Have no loose knots.
- Have no major splits.
- Have no decayed sections.
- Be suitable for turning and machining.
- Have sufficient structural strength.

## ⚙️ Proposed Fabrication Procedure

### Step 1 – Reference and Dimensioning

Measure the reference product or approve a dimensioned design concept.

The metal insert should be selected before machining the wooden body so that its seating and thermal separation can be properly designed.

### Step 2 – Wood Selection

Select a sound, seasoned wooden blank.

Mark:

- Centre line
- Overall length
- Maximum diameter
- Machining allowance

### Step 3 – Blank Preparation

Cut the wooden blank to the required size and secure it using an appropriate workshop holding method.

The setup should be checked before machining.

### Step 4 – Turning and Shaping

A wood lathe can be used to create the circular profile.

Shape:

- Base
- Central stem
- Upper housing

The original manufacturing process of the photographed product is not documented.

### Step 5 – Bowl Recess

Create the upper recess according to the selected metal insert.

The design should maintain:

- Proper bowl seating
- Adequate wooden thickness
- Required thermal separation
- Secure insert positioning

### Step 6 – Decorative Grooves

Create shallow circumferential grooves on the upper housing.

Care should be taken not to excessively reduce the structural thickness of:

- Upper housing
- Central stem

### Step 7 – Assembly

If the product is made using separate components, suitable joints and retention methods should be designed.

The supplied photographs do not establish whether the original product was manufactured as one piece or from multiple assembled parts.

### Step 8 – Finishing

Smooth the surfaces and edges.

Apply a suitable exterior finish while keeping the finish away from the hot zone.

### Step 9 – Inspection

Inspect:

- Dimensions
- Joints
- Surface finish
- Base flatness
- Bowl seating
- Stability
- Thermal separation

Thermal assessment should be completed before considering the product ready for heated service. 10
